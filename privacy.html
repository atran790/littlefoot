Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Overview

LittleFoot Baby ("the App") is published by Pomegranate Planning, LLC ("we", "us"). The App is built so your family's information stays on your device. We don't require an account. The one exception is the Product Scanner, which sends label photos through our scan server to OpenAI to read them, as described below.

Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the App stores the information you choose to enter, only on your device:

  • Your baby's profile: name, birthday, optional due date, optional sex, feeding approach, any food allergies you list, and an optional photo.
  • Your name and role, if you provide them.
  • Tracking logs: feedings, sleep, diapers, and growth measurements, plus any notes.
  • Favorites, notification preferences, and other settings.
  • Any feeding or sleep timer you start. While it runs, its start time can appear on your Lock Screen as a Live Activity.
  • Product scan results: label photos, label text, and results.

This information is not sent to us. It is not shared with anyone else, except as noted under AI Features and Product Scanner. It may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple manages under its own privacy policy.

Camera and Photos

The App uses the camera or your photo library only when you take or choose a photo: a profile photo or a product label scan. Profile photos stay on your device and are never uploaded. Product label photos are sent to OpenAI to read the label (see Product Scanner) and are saved in the App on your device. Links to brand websites and web searches in scan reports open in your browser only when you tap them.

AI Features

Product Scanner

When you scan a product, the App sends the label photos (up to 2) to our scan server, which passes them to OpenAI to read the label and translate it if needed. To write the report's summary, the App then sends, the same way: the label text; the product's name, brand and category; the result and the guidance it matched; your child's age in months (and corrected age, if you said they were born early); and, if the label lists something on your child's allergy list, that one allergen. It never sends your child's name, birthday or full allergy list. Our server doesn't store photos or label text. It keeps only what it needs to prevent abuse: an anonymous key for this install of the App, created by Apple's App Attest, and counts of scans (and of requests from the earlier features below) per day and per month, deleted within about 3 months. If you scan the same photos again within a day, the App reuses the earlier reading, which it keeps only in memory on your device.

OpenAI processes the photos and text under its API terms: it doesn't use them to train its models and keeps them for up to 30 days to monitor abuse, then deletes them, unless the law requires it to keep them longer. See OpenAI's privacy policy. Our scan server runs on Supabase.

Without a connection, phones with Apple Intelligence read the label on your device instead. If OpenAI can't write the summary, it may be written by Apple Private Cloud Compute, which Apple says uses data only to answer the request and doesn't store it, or on your device.

Text written by AI is marked in the App, and the App says which features use AI before you use them. Scan scores come from a rulebook based on US guidance, not from AI. Labels in other languages are scored from an English translation.

AI results are general information, not a medical diagnosis. Always contact your pediatrician with health concerns.

Earlier versions

Earlier versions of the app (2.0.3 and before) had Cry Analyzer and Diaper Check. Cry Analyzer sent sound measurements (never recordings), your baby's age and the time of day to OpenAI. Diaper Check sent color readings from the photo (never the photo), the texture and feeding type you picked, and your baby's age to OpenAI. Our server still counts those requests for older versions to limit cost. Results saved by those versions stay on your device, unused, until you delete the app.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The App checks your subscription status with Apple on your device.

Analytics and Advertising

The App does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Notifications

The App can send local reminders, such as daily activity tips, milestone alerts, and nap reminders. They are scheduled on your device and do not involve any server. You can turn them off in the App or in your device settings.

Third-Party Links

The Community button opens a Facebook group in your browser or the Facebook app. Anything you do there is governed by Facebook's privacy policy, not this one.

If You Live Outside the United States

The App is available in many countries and works the same way everywhere: what you enter stays on your device, and we don't receive it, except what a product scan sends as described above. This passes through our scan server (Supabase) to OpenAI, which may process them outside your country, including in the United States; we keep no photos or label text, only the anonymous install key and scan counts described above. You control everything else in the App and can delete it all by deleting the App. Under laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, you can still contact us with any privacy question or request at the address below, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Purchases and Private Cloud Compute are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

The App is for parents and caregivers and is not directed at children. Information you enter about your baby stays on your device, and we do not collect it. Product scans send label photos, label text, your child's age in months and the other scan details described above, never your child's name or birthday.

Your Choices and Deleting Your Data

You can edit or delete tracking entries in the App. To delete the App's data, delete the App from your device. You can turn off camera, photo, and notification access at any time in your device's Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact
