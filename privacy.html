Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Overview

LittleFoot Baby ("the App") is published by Pomegranate Planning, LLC ("we", "us"). The App is built so your family's information stays on your device. We don't run servers for the App, we don't require an account, and we never receive the information you enter.

Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the App stores the information you choose to enter, only on your device:

  • Your baby's profile: name, birthday, optional due date, optional sex, feeding approach, any food allergies you list, and an optional photo.
  • Your name and role, if you provide them.
  • Tracking logs: feedings, sleep, diapers, and growth measurements, plus any notes.
  • Favorites, notification preferences, and other settings.
  • Any feeding or sleep timer you start. While it runs, its start time can appear on your Lock Screen as a Live Activity.
  • Results from the AI features: cry analysis results, diaper check photos and results, and product label photos, label text, and results.

This information is not sent to us. It is not shared with anyone else, except as noted under AI Features. It may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple manages under its own privacy policy.

Microphone

The Cry Analyzer uses the microphone only while you are recording. The audio is analyzed in real time on your device and is never saved or sent anywhere. Only the analysis results are kept, on your device.

Camera and Photos

The App uses the camera or your photo library only when you take or choose a photo: a profile photo, a diaper check, or a product label scan. Photos are processed on your device and saved only in the App on your device. They are never uploaded. Links to brand websites and web searches in scan reports open in your browser only when you tap them.

AI Features

The Cry Analyzer and Diaper Check use Apple's on-device AI (Apple Intelligence). Your recordings, photos, and the results are processed on your device. We do not receive them.

The Product Scanner reads label photos on your device. On iOS 27 it may send the label text and your child's age and allergies (never photos) to Apple Private Cloud Compute to write its explanation. Apple says data sent to Private Cloud Compute is used only to answer the request, isn't stored, and can't be accessed by Apple. We never receive it.

Text written by AI is marked "AI" in the App, and the App tells you before its first AI feature runs. Scan scores come from a rulebook based on US guidance, not from AI. Labels in languages other than English aren't scored.

AI results are general information, not a medical diagnosis. Always contact your pediatrician with health concerns. Diaper Check isn't offered in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The App checks your subscription status with Apple on your device.

Analytics and Advertising

The App does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Notifications

The App can send local reminders, such as daily activity tips, milestone alerts, and nap reminders. They are scheduled on your device and do not involve any server. You can turn them off in the App or in your device settings.

Third-Party Links

The Community button opens a Facebook group in your browser or the Facebook app. Anything you do there is governed by Facebook's privacy policy, not this one.

If You Live Outside the United States

The App is available in many countries and works the same way everywhere: what you enter stays on your device, and we don't receive it. Because we don't collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side; you control it in the App and can delete it all by deleting the App. Under laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, you can still contact us with any privacy question or request at the address below, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Purchases and Private Cloud Compute are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

The App is for parents and caregivers and is not directed at children. Information you enter about your baby stays on your device, and we do not collect it.

Your Choices and Deleting Your Data

You can edit or delete tracking entries in the App. To delete the App's data, delete the App from your device. You can turn off microphone, camera, photo, and notification access at any time in your device's Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at renee@withlychee.com.
